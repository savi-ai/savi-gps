    Overview

    checkout-app is the customer purchase flow — cart, order orchestration, and payment capture — composed from three member repositories: storefront-web, checkout-api, and payments-service.

    What this application does

    storefront-web renders the cart and checkout UI and calls OrderController.java:118 ✓ in checkout-api to price and create the order. checkout-api authorizes and captures payment via PaymentGateway.go:76 ✓ in payments-service, which holds the PCI boundary for the application.

    Key Features

    • Server-priced cart and checkout UI, with session state owned by storefront-web
    • Order lifecycle orchestration and event publication owned by checkout-api
    • Payment authorization and capture isolated in payments-service's PCI-DSS zone
    • Async settlement reconciliation over Kafka between checkout-api and payments-service
